What to do

Picked for Stockholm specifically. No two cities here share a list.

Vasa Museum

A 1628 warship that sank on its maiden voyage and was raised in 1961 — the museum is built around it.

Cultural · 2 hours

Gamla Stan walking tour

The 13th-century old town on its own island — Royal Palace, Stortorget square, Mårten Trotzigs Gränd (the narrowest street).

Cultural · 2 hours

ABBA The Museum

A purpose-built museum on Djurgården — costumes, the original Polar Studio, karaoke booths.

Cultural · 1.5 hours

Archipelago day cruise

A six-hour boat through the Stockholm archipelago — Vaxholm, Grinda, lunch at a wooden island restaurant.

Outdoor · 6 hours
