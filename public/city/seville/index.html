What to do

Picked for Seville specifically. No two cities here share a list.

Real Alcázar

The Mudéjar royal palace where Game of Thrones filmed Dorne — book the early slot for the gardens alone.

Cultural · 2.5 hours

Cathedral + Giralda climb

The largest Gothic cathedral in the world — climb the 12th-century Giralda tower on its ramp (no stairs).

Cultural · 2 hours

Flamenco show + free tapas tour

A nightly flamenco show at Casa de la Memoria followed by a guide through three Triana tapas bars.

Cultural · 4 hours

Plaza de España + Parque María Luisa

The most-photographed plaza in Spain — a curved semicircle of tiled provinces, rowboats on the moat, all free.

Outdoor · 2 hours

Where to eat

Real places, with what they are known for.

El Rinconcillo

The oldest tapas bar in Spain (1670) — they still chalk your tab on the wooden counter. Order spinach with chickpeas.

Tapas · since 1670 · €€

Casa Morales

An 1850 wine shop turned tapas bar — order at the counter, eat standing, the cheapest jamón sandwich in the city.

Tapas · since 1850 · €

Mariscos Emilio

A standing-room seafood counter where you point at the day's fish — €10 grilled prawns, lunchtime only.

Seafood · standing-room · €€

Abantal

Julio Fernández's one-Michelin-star — the most refined Andalusian tasting in Seville. Skip the bistro for one night.

Modern Andalusian · 1 Michelin · €€€€
