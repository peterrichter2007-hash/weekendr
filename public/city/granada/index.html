What to do

Picked for Granada specifically. No two cities here share a list.

Alhambra + Generalife

The Nasrid Palaces, Generalife gardens and Alcazaba in one ticket — book three months ahead, take the early slot.

Cultural · 4 hours

Free tapas crawl

Granada's defining magic: every bar gives a free tapa with every drink. A guided crawl shows you the best route.

Food · 3 hours

Albaicín walking tour

A guided walk through the UNESCO-listed Albaicín — the carmen gardens, the Mirador San Nicolás for sunset.

Cultural · 2.5 hours

Hammam Al Ándalus Granada

Granada's Arab baths are the most historically accurate — three thermal pools, marble columns, mint tea.

Cultural · 1.5 hours
