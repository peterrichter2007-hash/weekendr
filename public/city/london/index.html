What to do

Picked for London specifically. No two cities here share a list.

British Museum

Free, world-class, the Rosetta Stone and the Parthenon marbles in one afternoon.

Cultural · 3 hours

Camden Market food crawl

Sixty stalls along Regent's Canal — bao, paella, brisket bagels, eat your way down the towpath.

Food · 2 hours

Sky Garden free observation

A free public garden at the top of 20 Fenchurch Street with a 360° view — book the slot two weeks ahead.

Outdoor · 1.5 hours

West End musical (matinee)

The TKTS booth on Leicester Square sells day-of West End tickets at 40-60% off — go for the matinee.

Cultural · 3 hours
