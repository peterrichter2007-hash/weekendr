What to do

Picked for Palma de Mallorca specifically. No two cities here share a list.

Palma Cathedral + Bishop's Palace

A 14th-century Gothic cathedral with a Gaudí-restored interior — go at noon when the rose-window light hits the back wall.

Cultural · 1.5 hours

Calas (hidden cove) day

Drive south-east for two of the cheapest, prettiest coves — Mondragó for sand, S'Almonia for cliff-jumping.

Beach · 6 hours

Soller train + Port de Sóller

A 1912 wooden train climbs over the Tramuntana mountains to the seaside town of Sóller — the most scenic hour on the island.

Outdoor · 5 hours

Bellver Castle + park

A 14th-century circular Gothic castle on a forested hill above Palma — Palma's best free panorama.

Cultural · 2 hours
